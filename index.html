Domača kuhinja zgornjega Posočja. Soška postrv, frika in bovški krafi, dve minuti hoje od viseče brvi čez Sočo. Home cooking from the upper Soča valley. Trout, frika and Bovec krafi, a two-minute walk from the suspension footbridge over the Soča.

Tri generacije, ena brv in recept za krafe, ki ga nona nikoli ni zapisala. Three generations, one footbridge, and a krafi recipe Nona never wrote down.

Nona Marija je leta 1962 v hiši ob brvi začela kuhati za drvarje, ki so hodili čez Sočo v gozd. Danes v kuhinji stoji njen vnuk Luka, goste pa sprejema Ana.

In 1962 Nona Marija started cooking for the woodcutters who crossed the Soča to work in the forest. Today her grandson Luka runs the kitchen and Ana looks after the guests.

Kuhamo preprosto in po sezoni. Postrv pride zjutraj, sir s planin, jurčke naberemo sami. Kar ni iz naše doline, je iz sosednje.

We cook simply and with the seasons. The trout arrives in the morning, the cheese comes down from the mountain pastures, and we pick the porcini ourselves. What isn't from our valley is from the next one.

Luka & Ana

Od izvira do krožnikaFrom source to plate

Dolina na krožnikuThe valley on your plate

Soča izvira v Trenti in teče mimo naše hiše proti morju. Večino sestavin dobimo ob njenem toku, od rejcev in pridelovalcev, ki jih poznamo po imenu. The Soča rises in Trenta and flows past our house on its way to the sea. Most of what we cook comes from along its course, from farmers and makers we know by name.
